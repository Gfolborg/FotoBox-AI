This commit consolidates fixes for various issues encountered during app startup and runtime, primarily stemming from environment inconsistencies and module resolution.

- **Comprehensive Dependency & Environment Cleanup:**

    - Executed `npx expo prebuild --clean` and performed a "nuclear" `node_modules` cleanup.

- **ESLint Configuration & Code Quality:**

This commit aims to provide a stable foundation by resolving a wide array of configuration, linting, and runtime issues.
